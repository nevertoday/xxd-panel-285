## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one scientific commemorative stamp-mark, not a travel souvenir and not a modern logo. Use a bold simplified silhouette, a little engraving line, halftone dots and uneven ink on paper. Color is strong vintage print: deep blue, warm red, orange, ivory, ink black or teal, taken from the photograph. Type is a compact archive number or short label. Reject a full scene, movie science fiction, neon, a commercial logo and tourist-souvenir styling.
